Put your background video here as: auth-bg.mp4

Recommended specs (this loads on the splash, login, and signup pages):
- H.264 MP4, 1080p or less
- 10-20 second loop, no audio track needed (it plays muted)
- Under ~4MB — this is on the pages people see before they even have an
  account, so keep it light
- Something subtle and slow-moving works best — it's a backdrop behind
  text and a form, not the main attraction

Free, properly-licensed sources: Pexels (pexels.com/videos) and Coverr
(coverr.co) both offer downloadable stock video under licenses that
permit this kind of use. Search something like "savings", "community",
"Nigeria", or an abstract/finance-adjacent theme.

If you skip this file entirely, the pages fall back to the poster image
(auth-bg.jpg in ../images/) with no video — everything still works.
